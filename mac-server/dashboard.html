Setup

1
Start the Mac server
Running — you're looking at it.
2
Load the Hex speech model
Starting…

This happens on its own. Hex needs to be installed in /Applications; the Hex app itself doesn't need to be open.

3
Install the iPhone app
Waiting for your iPhone…
  1. Plug in your iPhone and open ios/hex-ios-keyboard.xcodeproj in Xcode.
  2. Pick your iPhone at the top and press Run ▶.
  3. First time only: on the iPhone, Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → trust your Apple ID.
4
Connect your iPhone to this Mac
Not connected yet
  1. Make sure your iPhone is on the same Wi-Fi as this Mac.
  2. Open the Camera app on your iPhone and point it at this code.
  3. Tap the yellow Open in hex-ios-keyboard link that appears.
  4. Can't scan? In the app, tap Type the code instead and enter
5
Turn on the keyboard and dictate
Waiting for your first dictation…
  1. In the app, tap Open Settings → Keyboards → turn on hex-ios-keyboard and Allow Full Access.
  2. In any app, tap 🌐 to switch to hex-ios-keyboard.
  3. Hold the mic, talk, and let go. The text is typed for you, and the clip shows up in History below.
